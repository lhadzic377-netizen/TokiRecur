# Governance

## Maintainership

TokiRecur is maintained by a single maintainer: **Luke Hadzic**.

## Contributions

Every contribution — code, docs, or issues — is welcome and is governed by
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). The operating rule:

* Direct pushes to `main` are restricted to the maintainer.
* All changes land as pull requests reviewed and approved by the maintainer.
* The maintainer has final say on scope, design, and merge decisions.

This is a deliberate single-owner model while the project is young: one
reviewer keeps the review bar consistent and the codebase coherent.

## Releases

Versions follow SemVer. Version bumps are the maintainer's call. Significant
changes are recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
