The numbers

The benchmark figures were measured on the hardware named next to them, with the method described in the docs. They are measurements, not promises. Your hardware, your quantisation and your workload will give different numbers. The benchmark harness ships in the package so you can measure it yourself instead of taking this site's word for it.

Your models and your prompts

You run the models. The daemon routes requests to KV blocks that llama-server holds in memory, and keeps prompt metadata and embeddings in a local index; toki evict trims that index and toki down drops the KV state with the stack. If you point the daemon at a server you do not own, that operator receives the prompts, and this project is not responsible for what they do with them.
