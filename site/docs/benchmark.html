Benchmark

The benchmark compares baseline (no cache) vs recycled (cache enabled) runs across TTFT, prefill throughput, end-to-end latency, cache hit rate, and output equivalence, then prints a composite score (0–100) with a letter grade.

toki bench   # interactive: pick a model from the registry + a workload from a menu

# Fully scripted (registry name or path):
toki bench --model 9b --workload agent_long_context \
           --max-tokens 64 --output-json report.json

# Self-contained: spin up llama-server + bench together (auto-stop)
toki bench --model ~/Downloads/Qwen3.5-9B-Q4_K_M.gguf \
           --self-contained --ctx-size 8192 --n-gpu-layers 99

Scoring

The composite score (0–100) is derived from weights that reflect production importance rather than end-to-end wall-clock:

ComponentWeightWhat it measures
Prefill skip30%% of prefill tokens served from cache
TTFT improvement25%Reduction in time to first token
Throughput gain20%Prefill tokens/sec improvement
Cache hit rate15%Hit rate
Output equivalence10%Semantic similarity of outputs
Wall-clock gain5%Total latency reduction

Letter grades: A+ ≥ 90, A ≥ 80, B ≥ 70, C ≥ 60, D ≥ 50, F < 50.

The headline number is realistic_mixed: 216 prompts across 5 real-world categories (RAG, tool-using, multi-turn, support, one-off) — TTFT cut by two thirds, prefill nearly 6x faster, 88% cache hit rate, composite 84.4 (A). The summarization and code_review workloads are even stronger (A+). Low scores on no_overlap and the adversarial workloads are expected — zero-overlap prompts are the worst case for prefix caching, and the stress workload is designed to surface edge cases (false-positive hits from cosine similarity, minimal savings on short prompts, underperforming sequential growth).
